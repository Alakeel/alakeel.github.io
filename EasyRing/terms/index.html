Free to use

EasyRing is a free app. Download it and use it as much as you like, with no account, no ads, no trial period and no limits. Converting, compressing and editing images, PDFs and documents is free.

Pro is optional

Pro adds the video & audio tools, and PDF Toys on the Mac. It's an optional auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly.

The Pro subscription

Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play and charged to your Apple Account or Google Account. The price and period are shown in the app before you subscribe. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it. Cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play account settings, at least 24 hours before the current period ends; Pro then stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Apple and Google handle the payment; we never see your payment details or account. EasyRing asks the store whether your account has an active Pro subscription, so Pro works on each of your devices signed in to that account: use Restore purchase on the Pro page. When the subscription ends or is refunded, Pro turns off again.

Your files

EasyRing changes files only when you choose an action, and saves results as new files next to the originals. Keep backups of files that matter to you.
