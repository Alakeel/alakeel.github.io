Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Your files stay on your Mac

Every conversion, compression and edit happens on your Mac. EasyRing never uploads your files, their names or their contents.

No account, no ads, no tracking

EasyRing doesn't collect analytics, usage data or personal information, has no account and shows no ads. We don't use third-party SDKs that collect data.

Purchases

Pro is sold as an in-app purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. EasyRing asks Apple whether this Apple ID owns Pro, so it can unlock it and restore it on your other Macs.

In the version sold on our website, activating Pro sends your licence key to our store to check it, and the video & audio tools are downloaded once.

What's stored on your Mac

Your settings, your chosen apps and the options you use most are saved only on your Mac. Deleting EasyRing deletes them.

Permissions

macOS asks you to let EasyRing control Finder. EasyRing uses this only to read which files you've selected, and never changes anything in Finder. You can turn it off at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

Children

EasyRing collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
