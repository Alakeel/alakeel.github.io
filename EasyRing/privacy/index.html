Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Your files stay on your device

Every conversion, compression and edit happens on your Mac, iPhone, iPad or Android device. EasyRing never uploads your files, their names or their contents.

No account, no tracking

EasyRing doesn't collect analytics, usage data or personal information, and has no account. We don't use third-party SDKs that collect data.

What's stored on your device

Your settings, your chosen apps and the options you use most are saved only on your device. Deleting EasyRing deletes them.

Permissions

macOS asks you to let EasyRing control Finder. EasyRing uses this only to read which files you've selected, and never changes anything in Finder. You can turn it off at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

On iPhone, iPad and Android, EasyRing opens only the photos, videos and files you pick, saves results to your photo library when you ask it to, and uses the camera and microphone only if you choose to take a photo or video to convert.

Children

EasyRing collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
