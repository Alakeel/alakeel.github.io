Help, ideas or a bug? Email alakeel.lab@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within two working days.

The wheel doesn't show my files

EasyRing needs permission to read your Finder selection. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation, find EasyRing and turn on Finder. Then select files in Finder before holding the key.

Change the keys

Open EasyRing from the menu bar → Settings → Shortcut, click a wheel's shortcut and hold the keys you want.

Uninstall

Quit EasyRing from its menu bar icon, then move EasyRing from Applications to the Trash.
