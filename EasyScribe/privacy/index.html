Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

EasyScribe collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We never see your recordings, transcripts or notes.

Your recordings and transcripts

Recording, transcription, speaker labels and the AI notes all happen on your iPhone or iPad. Your audio, transcripts and notes are saved in EasyScribe's own storage on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

The AI notes (summary, key points, action items, clean up, translate and questions) use Apple Intelligence on your device, or a Qwen3 model if you download one in Settings. When neither is available, simple built-in tools are used instead, also on your device.

You decide how long things stay: delete a recording at any time, keep only the text with Delete audio after transcribing, or let older recordings go after 30 or 90 days with Auto-delete recordings (favourites are always kept). Deleting the app removes everything stored in it.

Network

EasyScribe has no servers. It only goes online for these:

  • Speech models. To write down what you say offline, EasyScribe downloads a Whisper speech model once from Hugging Face (the Tiny model, 75 MB, on first launch; other models, including the Parakeet and Qwen3-ASR speech engines and the Qwen3 AI models, only if you choose them in Settings). If you ask it to find speakers, it also downloads a small speaker detection model (about 22 MB) from Hugging Face, only then. These are plain file downloads: nothing about you or your recordings is sent. After that, everything works offline.
  • Apple's own downloads. If you use Apple's speech engine or Apple Intelligence, iOS itself may download language packs or models from Apple.
  • Pro. If you subscribe, the purchase goes through Apple's App Store, and EasyScribe asks the App Store whether your subscription is active. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account.
  • Links you tap, such as this website, open in your browser.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record when you tap the record button.
  • Speech recognition: to turn your recordings into text with Apple's speech engine, on your device only.
  • Face ID: only if you turn on Lock with Face ID. EasyScribe never sees your face data; iOS only tells it whether the check passed.
  • Photos and files: only the video or file you pick to import.

Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

Recordings made on Apple Watch go straight to your iPhone, which transcribes them like any other recording. The widgets, Control Center control, Live Activity, Siri and Shortcuts only start a recording or show your latest summary on your own device.

Children

EasyScribe collects no data from anyone, including children.
