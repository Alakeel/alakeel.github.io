Meetings, lectures, interviews and quick ideas, turned into text you can search and share.

🎙️

One tap to record

One big button to record, pause, resume and stop, with a live waveform and timer. It keeps going when you switch apps and pauses for calls.

📝

Live transcript

Your words appear as you speak. Tap any line to hear that moment again, or long-press it to fix a word.

🌍

About 99 languages

Arabic, English and many more, using Apple's speech engines or Whisper. Auto-detect handles recordings that mix languages, such as Arabic and English.

✨

AI notes

A summary, key points and action items with who and when. Clean up the text, translate it, or ask a question about the recording. Made on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence or a Qwen3 model you download.

👥

Who said what

Each line gets a speaker: Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on, each in its own colour. Rename them to real names, and the names follow into exports.

📥

Import audio and video

Pick a video from Photos, or any audio or video file (MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, MOV and more), and EasyScribe writes it out in the background.

📤

Export anywhere

Markdown, plain text, or SRT and WebVTT subtitles, with times and speaker names. Or share the audio, or copy the text.

🗂️

Stay organised

Folders, tags and favourites. Search every title and transcript. Play back at 0.75× to 2×.

⌚

Record from anywhere

A Home Screen and Lock Screen widget, a Control Center button, Siri and Shortcuts, a Live Activity while you record, and an Apple Watch app.

Free and private

EasyScribe records, transcribes and writes your notes on your iPhone. Your recordings are never uploaded.

  • Free to use, with recordings up to 15 minutes each
  • Pro, monthly or yearly: longer recordings, speaker labels, Ask, Email, Remove noise, SRT and WebVTT subtitles, High and Ultra audio
  • No account and no tracking
  • Works offline once the speech model is on your phone
  • Lock it with Face ID
