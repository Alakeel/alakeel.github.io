Terms of Use

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Free to use

EasyScribe is a free app, with no account. Recording (up to 15 minutes per recording), transcribing, the AI notes and exporting are free.

Pro is optional

Pro adds recordings longer than 15 minutes, speaker labels, Ask and Email, Remove noise, SRT and WebVTT subtitles, and High and Ultra recording quality. It's an optional auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly.

The Pro subscription

Pro is bought through the App Store and charged to your Apple Account. The price and period are shown in the app before you subscribe. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it. Cancel anytime in your App Store account settings, at least 24 hours before the current period ends; Pro then stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or account. EasyScribe asks the App Store whether your Apple Account has an active Pro subscription, so Pro works on each of your devices signed in to that account: use Restore purchase on the Pro page. When the subscription ends or is refunded, Pro turns off again; your recordings and transcripts stay.

Recording other people

Only record people when you are allowed to. In many places you need everyone's permission before recording a conversation, a meeting or a call. You're responsible for what you record with EasyScribe and how you share it.

Your content

Your recordings, transcripts and notes are yours. Only import audio and video you have the right to use.

Transcripts and AI notes

Transcripts, speaker labels, summaries, action items and translations are made automatically and can contain mistakes. Check anything important against the recording before you rely on it.

Your files

Recordings are saved on your device only. Deleting a recording or the app removes it from the device, and auto-delete removes older recordings if you turn it on. Keep backups of recordings that matter to you.

Fair use

Don't copy, modify, reverse engineer or redistribute EasyScribe.

EasyScribe is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for lost recordings, wrong transcripts or other damages from using it.
