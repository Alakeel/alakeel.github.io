Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Nothing to buy and no account.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, plus an Apple Watch app for recording on your wrist.

Does it work offline?

Yes. On first launch EasyScribe downloads its speech model once (142 MB). You can record while it downloads, and the text is written when it finishes. If you're offline, it tries again next time you open the app. After that, recording, transcribing and the AI notes all work in airplane mode.

Which languages can it write down?

About 99 with the Whisper engine, including Arabic and English. Pick the language at the top of the record screen. Apple's speech engine supports the languages on Apple's list.

I speak Arabic and English in the same recording.

Pick Auto-detect (Whisper) at the top of the language list. Whisper works out the language of each part on its own. It works best when each language lasts a sentence or two; switching in the middle of a sentence is hit-and-miss. For Arabic, the Small or Large v3 Turbo models do much better than Tiny.

Which speech engine should I use?

Settings → Speech engine. Whisper (the default) works in about 99 languages and can auto-detect. Apple uses your iPhone's own speech engine. Auto uses Apple where it can and Whisper otherwise. All of them run on your phone.

How do I get a more accurate transcript?

Choose a bigger Whisper model in Settings: Tiny (75 MB, fastest), Base (142 MB, balanced), Small (466 MB, more accurate) or Large v3 Turbo (547 MB, most accurate, for recent iPhones). Then use Transcribe again on a recording to redo it.

It keeps spelling a name wrong.

Add it to Settings → Speech recognition → Custom words, spelled the way you want, for example Alakeel, Riyadh. Keep the list short (10 to 30 words). It helps new recordings, imports and Transcribe again.

How do I fix a line in the transcript?

Long-press the line to edit its text or change its speaker. The time stays the same. Tap a line to jump the audio to that moment.

How do speaker labels work?

After a recording is transcribed, each line gets Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on. Long-press a name to rename it everywhere. If voices sound alike, tap ⋯ → Find speakers and tell it how many people (2 to 5) were talking. You can turn labels off in Settings → Transcripts.

Why are my AI notes so basic?

The full AI notes use Apple Intelligence, on an iPhone 15 Pro or later with iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in the iPhone's Settings. Without it, EasyScribe uses simpler built-in tools, and its Settings tell you why.

Can I transcribe a video or an audio file?

Yes. Import a video from Photos, or any audio or video file (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, MP4, MOV and more). It's transcribed in the background.

How do I export a transcript?

Open the recording and tap ⋯ → Export transcript. Choose Markdown (with the summary, key points and action items), plain text, or SRT or WebVTT subtitles. Times and speaker names are included.

How do I organise recordings?

Long-press a recording to add it to a folder, give it tags or star it as a favourite. The chips above the list filter by folder, tag or favourites, and search looks through titles and transcripts too.

Can I start recording without opening the app?

Yes: add the EasyScribe widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, add the Record control to Control Center or the Action Button (iOS 18 or later), or say "Start recording in EasyScribe" to Siri. While recording, the timer shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

How does the Apple Watch app work?

Record on your watch; the audio is sent to your iPhone, which transcribes it like any other recording. Recordings wait on the watch until your iPhone takes them.

Can I see my recordings on my iPad too?

Not yet: recordings stay on the device you made them on. To move one, use ⋯ → Share audio or Export transcript and open it on the other device.

How do I lock the app?

Turn on Lock with Face ID in Settings. EasyScribe then asks for Face ID when it opens and when you come back to it.

EasyScribe is using a lot of space.

Settings shows the storage used by recordings and speech models. Clear models you don't need, turn on Delete audio after transcribing to keep only the text, or let old recordings go after 30 or 90 days with Auto-delete (favourites are always kept).

Does it keep recording if I switch apps?

Yes. It keeps going in the background and pauses for phone calls.
