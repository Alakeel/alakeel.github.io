Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

EasyCut collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Your videos and photos

Everything you edit is processed on your phone. The photos, videos and music you add are copied into EasyCut's own storage on your device so your projects keep working, and they are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting a project, or the app, removes them.

Permissions

  • Photos: to open the videos and photos you pick, and to save your exports when you tap Save to Photos.
  • Camera and microphone (iPhone): only if a future version lets you film a clip from inside the app; EasyCut never uses them on its own.

Network

EasyCut works fully offline. The only network use is opening links you tap (this website) in your browser.

Children

EasyCut collects no data from anyone, including children.
