Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription and no watermark.

The export failed.

Try a lower resolution (1080p instead of 4K) or the MP4 format. Very long reversed clips use a lot of memory; trim them first. If it keeps failing, email us your phone model and the export settings.

Where are my projects?

On the home screen, under Projects. They are saved automatically as you edit. Long-press a project to rename, duplicate or delete it.

How do I move text or stickers?

Tap it on the preview, then drag with one finger, or pinch and twist with two. It snaps to the centre lines.

How do I reorder clips?

Long-press a clip in the timeline and drag it to its new place, or use Reorder in the clip tools.

How do I crop a video?

Select the clip, tap Crop, then drag the box or pick a shape. For a single video without a project, use the Crop quick tool.

Can I add my own music?

Yes: Audio → Music or sound file, or Sound from a video.
