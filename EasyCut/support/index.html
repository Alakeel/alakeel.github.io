Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription and no watermark.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Editing, exporting and every quick tool run on your phone, even in airplane mode. Your videos are never uploaded.

Where are my projects?

On the home screen, under Projects. They are saved automatically as you edit. Long-press a project to rename, duplicate or delete it.

How do I trim or split a clip?

Tap the clip in the timeline, then drag its handles to trim. To split, move the playhead to the spot and tap Split.

How do I reorder clips?

Long-press a clip in the timeline and drag it to its new place, or use Reorder in the clip tools.

How do I move text or stickers?

Tap it on the preview, then drag with one finger, or pinch and twist with two. It snaps to the centre lines.

How do I add filters or transitions?

Select a clip and tap Filters to pick one of 22 looks and set its strength, or Adjust for brightness, colour and more. For a transition, select a clip and tap Transition.

How do I crop a video?

Select the clip, tap Crop, then drag the box or pick a shape. For a single video without a project, use the Crop quick tool.

How do I change the speed?

Select the clip and tap Speed: anything from 0.1× slow motion to 8× time lapse. The sound keeps its pitch.

Can I add my own music?

Yes: Audio → Music or sound file, or Sound from a video. You can set the volume and fade it in and out.

How do I make a video smaller?

Open Quick tools → Compress, pick a video, then choose Light, Medium or Strong, or set your own size. EasyCut shows the size before and after.

How do I convert a video?

Quick tools → Convert. Choose MP4, HEVC or WEBM for video, GIF, or MP3, M4A or WAV for sound only.

How do I get just the sound?

Quick tools → Extract audio saves the sound as MP3, M4A or WAV.

Can I make a GIF?

Yes. Quick tools → Video to GIF, at 320, 480 or 640 pixels wide, or your own size.

What else can the quick tools do?

Mute or halve the sound, play a video backwards, lower its resolution, crop it to a new shape, rotate or mirror it, and make it faster or slow motion. Each one is a single tap.

Where do quick tool results go?

When it's done, tap Open to play the result, save it to Photos or share it to any app.

Which export settings should I use?

1080p MP4 at 30 fps suits most apps. Choose 4K for the best quality, HEVC for smaller files on newer phones, or GIF for short loops.

The export failed.

Try a lower resolution (1080p instead of 4K) or the MP4 format. Very long reversed clips use a lot of memory; trim them first. If it keeps failing, email us your phone model and the export settings.
