Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

The tools people actually use, made big enough for your thumb.

✂️

Trim, split, reorder

A timeline that scrolls under a fixed playhead. Drag handles to trim, tap Split, long-press and drag to reorder. Pinch or tap to zoom down to a tenth of a second.

🎨

Filters & adjust

22 looks, from Vivid to Noir, plus brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, tint, vignette and sharpen. Live on the preview.

🔤

Text & stickers

Neon, outline, box and shadow styles, any colour. Drag, pinch and spin into place, with slide and fade animations.

🖼️

Video in video

Put a second clip or photo on top, picture-in-picture, with its own filter, opacity, animation and sound.

✨

20+ transitions

Fade, flash, wipes, slides, circle, clock, pixels, zoom and more, between any two clips or all at once.

🎵

Music & sound

Add songs or the sound from another video, detach a clip's audio, set volume and fades. Speed changes keep the pitch.

⚡

Speed, reverse, freeze

0.1× to 8× slow motion and time lapse, play clips backwards, or freeze a frame for a beat.

🎬

Crop & animate

Crop any clip to a free or fixed shape, and give it a fade, zoom or slide on the way in and out.

📐

Any shape

9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3 or 21:9, on a blurred, black, white or coloured background.

🚀

Export up to 4K

480p to 4K, 24–60 fps, MP4, HEVC or GIF, using your phone's hardware encoder. No watermark, ever.

Three steps

From camera roll to finished video in a minute.

Pick

Tap New project and choose your videos and photos, in order.

Edit

Cut, add filters, text, stickers, music and overlays. Undo anything.

Export

Choose the size, save to Photos or share straight to any app.
