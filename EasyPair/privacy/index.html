Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026

EasyPair collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We never see your files, your texts or the devices you connect.

Your files and texts

Files and texts shared through EasyPair are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. Devices you connect read and send them directly over your local network. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else.

Files and texts stay only on your phone. They are deleted when you delete them in EasyPair, or when you delete the app. On iPhone, received files also show in the Files app under On My iPhone → EasyPair.

Network

EasyPair has no servers and makes no network requests to any server. It runs a small web server on your phone that only devices on the same Wi-Fi, or joined to your phone's hotspot, can reach. It works without internet. Links you tap, such as this website, open in your browser.

Who can get in

Other devices need the 6-digit PIN to log in. Five wrong tries lock that device out for a while. You can see every device, remove any of them, which logs it out at once, or log out every device and pick a new PIN.

The hub uses plain HTTP on your local network, so traffic is not encrypted. Don't use EasyPair on a Wi-Fi network you don't trust; use your phone's Personal Hotspot instead.

Permissions

  • Local Network (iPhone): lets nearby devices reach the hub on your phone.
  • Photos: only the items you pick to add. EasyPair doesn't browse or read the rest of your library.
  • Files: only the files you pick to add.

Children

EasyPair collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
