Transfer files from phone to computer.
No cable. No internet.

EasyPair is a free app for iPhone and Android that turns your phone into a file hub. Any phone, tablet or computer nearby opens your phone's address in a browser, enters a PIN, and can send and get files. Nothing to install on them, no account, no cloud.

Open. Enter the PIN. Share.

Photos from a friend's phone, documents to your laptop, a link to the tablet: all through your phone.

🌐

Nothing to install

Any phone, tablet or computer with a browser can join. Open the phone's address, or scan the QR code on the phone.

🔢

A PIN to get in

Devices log in with a 6-digit PIN. Show it, copy it or make a new one in the Hub tab.

📡

No internet needed

Use the same Wi-Fi, your phone's Personal Hotspot with no router, or a USB cable. Files never go through the internet.

📤

Drop in any file

Drag and drop or paste files on the page. Send several at once with progress and speed. Any size: files stream straight to storage.

🖼️

Preview in the browser

Open photos, videos, audio, PDFs and text right on the page. Download one file, or several as one ZIP.

🔎

Find it fast

Grid or list with thumbnails, search and sort. Filter by photos, videos, audio, documents or archives.

💬

Texts and links

Share a link, a note or a Wi-Fi password with every device, and copy it there with one tap.

🟢

See who's online

The device list shows who's connected. New files show up on every page as they arrive.

⚙️

Your rules

Choose what visitors can do: upload, delete and rename, share texts. Pick a theme (White, Blue or Black) and English or Arabic.

Three steps

From your phone to any screen nearby in seconds.

Turn on

Open EasyPair and turn on the hub. It shows your address and PIN.

Open

On the other device, open the address in a browser, or scan the QR code.

Share

Enter the PIN, then send, preview and download files and texts.

Three ways to connect, no router needed

The other devices just need to reach your phone. No internet at all.

📶

Same Wi-Fi

Phone and devices on the same Wi-Fi network. EasyPair shows the address for each network.

🔥

Personal Hotspot

No Wi-Fi around? Turn on your phone's hotspot and let the other devices join it. No router needed.

🔌

USB cable

The fastest way: plug the phone into the computer and use its hotspot over USB. Bluetooth through the hotspot works too, just slower.

Free and private

EasyPair keeps everything on your phone and your local network. No servers, nothing goes to the internet.

  • Free to download and use
  • No account, no analytics, no tracking
  • Only devices with the PIN get in, and you can remove any of them
  • Five wrong PINs lock that device out for a while
