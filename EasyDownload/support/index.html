Which links work?

Direct links to a file: the address usually ends in .mp4, .mov, .mp3, .m4a, .jpg, .png, .pdf, .zip and so on, and opening it in a browser starts a download or shows just that file. Only secure links (https://) can be downloaded.

Why can't I download from YouTube, Instagram or TikTok?

Those sites (and others like Facebook, X, Vimeo and Spotify) don't allow downloading their videos and posts in their terms, and the App Store and Google Play don't allow apps that do it. Their links open a player, not a file, so EasyDownload tells you right away instead of saving a web page.

"This link opens a web page, not a file"

The link leads to a page that shows the file rather than to the file itself. Open the page, look for its Download button, and copy that link instead (long-press it and choose Copy Link).

Where are my downloads?

In the Library tab. On iPhone and iPad they're also in the Files app: On My iPhone → EasyDownload. On Android, use ⋯ → Save to a folder, or Share to send a file to another app. Photos and videos can go to your photo library with Save to Photos.

Converting to MP3, smaller videos and GIFs

Pick the format in the sheet that appears after you tap Get download. If a file can't be converted, the original is kept.
