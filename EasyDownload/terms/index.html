Terms of Use

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Free to use

EasyDownload is a free app. Downloading files in their original format is free, with no account, no ads and no limits on how much you download.

Pro is optional

Pro adds converting (MP3, M4A, smaller MP4, GIF) and downloading several links at once. It's an optional, one-time in-app purchase through the App Store or Google Play: no subscription, nothing charged automatically, and it never expires. Future Pro features are included.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple or Google, who handle the payment. EasyDownload asks the store whether your account owns Pro, so it can unlock it and restore it on your other devices.

What you download

Only download files you have the right to save, and respect the terms of the sites you download from. You're responsible for what you download and how you use it. EasyDownload doesn't download from sites that don't allow it, such as YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Your files

Files are saved on your device. Keep backups of files that matter to you.
