Usage notice

EasyDownload works with public, direct download links. Private, protected, or sign-in-required content is not supported, except X and Instagram posts your own account can see: you can sign in to X or Instagram inside the app. That sign-in stays inside the app and is only used to fetch the posts you ask for.

Please respect copyright and download only files you own or have permission to save. You are responsible for the content you download and how you use it.

Free to use

EasyDownload is a free app, with no account and no ads. Downloading, converting and videos up to 480p are free.

Pro is optional

Pro adds videos above 480p: 720p, 1080p and 4K. It's an optional auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly.

The Pro subscription

Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play and charged to your Apple Account or Google Account. The price and period are shown in the app before you subscribe. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it. Cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play account settings, at least 24 hours before the current period ends; Pro then stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

Apple and Google handle the payment; we never see your payment details or account. EasyDownload asks the store whether your account has an active Pro subscription, so Pro works on each of your devices signed in to that account: use Restore purchase on the Pro page. When the subscription ends or is refunded, Pro turns off again and videos download at up to 480p.

What you download

Only download files you have the right to save, and respect the terms of the sites you download from. You're responsible for what you download and how you use it. YouTube links aren't supported.

Your files

Files are saved on your device. Keep backups of files that matter to you.
