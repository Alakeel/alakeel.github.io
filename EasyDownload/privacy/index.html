Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Usage notice

EasyDownload works with public, direct download links. Private, protected, or sign-in-required content is not supported, except X and Instagram posts your own account can see: you can sign in to X or Instagram inside the app. That sign-in stays inside the app and is only used to fetch the posts you ask for.

Please respect copyright and download only files you own or have permission to save. You are responsible for the content you download and how you use it.

Your files stay on your phone

EasyDownload saves files straight from the link you give it to your iPhone, iPad or Android phone. Converting happens on the device too. We never receive your links, your files or their names.

No account, no ads, no tracking

EasyDownload doesn't collect analytics, usage data or personal information, has no account and shows no ads. It contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Network

The app connects only to the address in the link you paste, to download that file. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

X and Instagram sign-in

Only if you choose to sign in to X or Instagram for a post that needs it, the sign-in page opens inside the app and that site keeps you signed in there, on your phone. EasyDownload uses it only to fetch the posts you ask for, straight from X or Instagram; we never see your username, password or account. Settings → Clear browsing data signs you out.

What's stored on your phone

Your downloads, the list of where they came from, and your settings are kept only on your phone. Files you move to the Secret vault stay on your phone too, and its password is checked on your phone and never leaves it. Deleting EasyDownload deletes them. On iPhone and iPad your downloads also appear in the Files app under On My iPhone → EasyDownload.

Permissions

Photos: only when you choose Save to Photos (or turn on "Also save to Photos"), EasyDownload asks to add the photos and videos you downloaded to your library. It doesn't read your existing photos. No other permission is used: no camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

EasyDownload collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
