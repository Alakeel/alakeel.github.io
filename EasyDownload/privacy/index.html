Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Your files stay on your phone

EasyDownload saves files straight from the link you give it to your iPhone, iPad or Android phone. Converting happens on the device too. We never receive your links, your files or their names.

No account, no ads, no tracking

EasyDownload doesn't collect analytics, usage data or personal information, has no account and shows no ads. It contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Network

The app connects only to the address in the link you paste, to download that file. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

What's stored on your phone

Your downloads, the list of where they came from, and your settings are kept only on your phone. Deleting EasyDownload deletes them. On iPhone and iPad your downloads also appear in the Files app under On My iPhone → EasyDownload.

Permissions

Photos: only when you choose Save to Photos (or turn on "Also save to Photos"), EasyDownload asks to add the photos and videos you downloaded to your library. It doesn't read your existing photos. No other permission is used: no camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

EasyDownload collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
