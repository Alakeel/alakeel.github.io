EasyTone is a free app for iPhone and iPad. Write your email however it comes out, angry, rushed or unsure. See how it sounds, pick a tone, and get the email you meant to send, all made on your iPhone. No account, nothing uploaded.

Write. Check. Send.

Complaints, follow-ups, apologies and thank-yous, in words that land the way you mean them.

🌡️

See how it sounds

A live meter shows warmth, formality, anger and clarity as you type, with the closest tone as an emoji and one line on how it will come across.

🪄

Tips that help

Clear suggestions to make it better: start with a greeting, swap blame for facts, say what you want and by when.

😌

Pick the tone

Friendly, Polite, Professional, Firm, Upset, Sorry, Thankful, Excited, Calm, Diplomatic or Short. Then Gentle, Clear or Strong.

✨

Rewritten on your iPhone

Apple Intelligence rewrites the email on your iPhone and keeps every fact, name, date and amount. Nothing is uploaded.

📄

Scan any letter

Scan a letter with the camera, or pick a screenshot. The text is read on your iPhone, ready to answer.

📋

Paste or type

Paste from any app, or type straight in. Add a subject if you like, and your name to sign with.

📨

Send it

Edit the result, copy it, share it or open it in Mail, with a quick check of how it sounds now.

🗂️

Ready-made templates

16 starting points: sick day, late delivery, refund, noisy neighbour, wrong bill, thank you and more.

🕘

History

Every rewrite is kept on your iPhone. Search it, reopen one, or swipe to delete.

Three steps

From a hot-headed draft to a calm, clear email in a minute.

Check

Send
