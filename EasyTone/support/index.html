Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It is free to download and use, with no account.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later. The rewrites with Apple Intelligence need a supported iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later; everything else works on all of them.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The tone check, the rewrites and scanning all run on your iPhone, so they work in airplane mode.

How do I rewrite an email?

Write, paste or scan your email, pick a tone in the Make it sound row (for example Calm) and a strength (Gentle, Clear or Strong), then tap Rewrite. Edit the result if you like, then copy it, share it or open it in Mail.

What does the meter show?

How your email will come across: warmth, formality, anger and clarity from 0 to 100, the closest tone as an emoji, and tips to make it better. It updates as you type; you can turn that off in Settings. Tap Check with Apple Intelligence for the on-device model's opinion.

Why are my rewrites so basic?

The full rewrites use Apple Intelligence, on an iPhone 15 Pro or later (or a supported iPad) with iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. Without it, EasyTone uses a simpler built-in rewrite, and its Settings tell you why.

Will it change my facts?

It's asked to keep every fact, name, date and amount. Rewrites are made automatically, so give the result a quick read before you send it.

Which languages does it understand?

The app is in 12 languages: English, Arabic, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi, Japanese, German, French, Korean, Russian and Turkish. Apple Intelligence rewrites in the same language as your email, for the languages it supports. Scanning reads the languages Apple's text recognition supports, listed in Settings. The built-in checker works best with English.

How do I scan a letter?

Tap Scan and hold your iPhone over the letter; you can scan several pages. To read a screenshot or a photo instead, tap Photo. The text is read on your iPhone and put in the email box.

How do templates work?

Open Templates, pick one (sick day, late delivery, refund, thank you and more), fill in the [brackets], then choose a tone and tap Rewrite.

Where are my old rewrites?

In History, saved on your iPhone only. Search them, tap one to reopen it, or swipe left to delete it. Settings can clear it all.

How do I sign emails with my name?

Add your name in Settings → Writing (or on the last page of the welcome tour). Rewrites end with it.

Can I set a default tone?

Yes: Settings → Writing lets you pick the tone and strength EasyTone starts with.

How do I see the example again?

Settings → App → Load the example email, or Show the welcome tour.
