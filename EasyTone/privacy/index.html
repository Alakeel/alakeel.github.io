Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

EasyTone collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We never see your emails, scans or rewrites.

Your emails and rewrites

The tone check, the tips and the rewrites all happen on your iPhone or iPad. Rewrites use Apple Intelligence on your device; EasyTone never uses Apple's Private Cloud Compute. When Apple Intelligence is off or not available, a built-in checker and a simple rewrite are used instead, also on your device.

Your history of rewrites, your name for signing emails and your settings are saved in EasyTone's own storage on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Delete any rewrite from History at any time, or clear it all. Deleting the app removes everything stored in it.

Network

EasyTone has no servers and works offline. It only goes online for these:

  • Apple's own downloads. If you use Apple Intelligence, iOS itself may download its models from Apple.
  • Sending an email. When you tap Open in Mail or Share, the email goes to the app you pick, which sends it the way it normally does.
  • Links you tap, such as this website, open in your browser.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you tap Scan, to scan a letter or document and read its text on your device.
  • Photos: only the photo or screenshot you pick, to read its text on your device.

Children

EasyTone collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
