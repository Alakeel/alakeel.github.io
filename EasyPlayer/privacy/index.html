Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

EasyPlayer collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We never see your videos or what you watch.

Your videos

When you import a video, EasyPlayer saves a copy in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad, with a small thumbnail and details such as its length and resolution. Playback, thumbnails and everything else happen on your device. Your videos are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Deleting a video in EasyPlayer removes its copy and thumbnail from the app; the original in Photos or Files is never touched. Deleting the app removes everything stored in it.

Network

EasyPlayer has no servers and makes no network requests. It works the same in airplane mode. Links you tap, such as this website, open in your browser.

Permissions

  • Photos: only the videos you pick in the photo picker to import. EasyPlayer doesn't browse or read the rest of your library.
  • Files: only the files you pick to import.

Ratings

EasyPlayer may ask once whether you'd like to rate it, using Apple's own review sheet. What you choose there goes to Apple, not to us.

Children

EasyPlayer collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
