Many videos.
One screen.

EasyPlayer is a free video player for iPhone and iPad that plays up to nine videos at once, each with its own sound. Pick a grid, shuffle the videos, drag them around, and tap any one to watch it full size. No account, works offline.

Watch. Shuffle. Swap.

Dance routines, travel clips, sports angles, the kids' videos: all of them at once.

🎬

Up to nine at once

Pick a grid of 1, 2 (side by side or stacked), 3, 4, 6 or 9 videos. Pick fewer and the grid fits them.

🔊

Every sound, mixed

Every video plays its own sound, all mixed together. Mute one with its speaker button, or all of them at once.

🔀

Shuffle the wall

Shuffle which video plays where. Each video gets its own random start, so no two show the same moment.

✋

Drag to swap

Hold a video and drop it on another to swap them. They keep playing; nothing reloads.

🔍

Tap one to focus

Open any video full size to seek, jump 10 seconds or change the speed. Tap Start here to set where it starts, then go back to the grid.

🎲

Start where you like

From the beginning, or shuffled with random starts. Set where each video starts, or play another one in its place from its ⋯ menu.

📱

Just the videos

Hide the controls with one tap, or let them fade on their own. Turn the phone sideways for landscape full screen.

🗂️

Your library

Import several videos at once from Photos or Files. List or Grid view, search, select several, with length, resolution and a 4K badge.

⚙️

Your way

Choose the default grid, start muted, loop, lines between videos, a progress line on each video, and light or dark.

Three steps

From your camera roll to a wall of videos in seconds.

Import

Pick videos from Photos or Files, several at once.

Play

Tap Play all, or pick the ones you want, and choose a grid.

Arrange

Shuffle, swap, mute and focus until the wall is just right.

Free and private

EasyPlayer plays your videos on your iPhone and nowhere else. It never goes online.

  • Free to download and use
  • No account and no tracking
  • Works offline, always
  • Your originals in Photos and Files are never touched
